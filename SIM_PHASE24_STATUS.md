# BANDALYTICS Full Sim — Phase 24

## Added
- Fixed historical joint replay protocol (`FIXED_LINEUP_POSITION_V1`) based only on batting-order slots and predefined markets; no result or model-output selection is allowed.
- 12 standardized 2- and 3-leg combination templates across leadoff, 3-hole, cleanup, moneyline, fixed 3.5 team totals, and fixed 8.5 game total events.
- Date-range replay batch CLI that reads historical date-addressed lineups, calls the strict Phase 23 replay route, settles automatically, and writes a calibration batch JSON.
- Batch summary + valid-record extraction utilities. Invalid, pushed, failed, or unsettled responses are excluded from calibration records.
- Protocol and batch regression tests, including an explicit proof that outcome fields do not alter generated combinations.

## Why fixed protocol matters
Calibration must not be generated by choosing combinations after seeing results or cherry-picking model outputs. The protocol is declared before settlement and is stable across games.

## Postseason boundary

2026 postseason data is a separate research sample. It does **not** count toward the unfinished 2026 regular-season 5/10 canary gate because postseason pitcher deployment, bullpen leverage, roster usage, lineup behavior, and market conditions differ from the regular season.

Postseason work may be used to:

- test pipeline integrity;
- study whether signals behave differently in playoff baseball;
- collect descriptive evidence;
- discover hypotheses for later development work.

It may **not** be used to retroactively complete the regular-season forward gate or to claim regular-season profitability.

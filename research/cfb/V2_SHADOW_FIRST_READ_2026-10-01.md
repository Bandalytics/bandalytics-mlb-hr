## Adjacent-key comparison

The shadow run does **not** support treating all key crossings as interchangeable. Adjacent classes were materially less stable season to season:

- Key 3 stayed close to random across most seasons.
- Key 7 was inconsistent and included multiple sub-50% seasons.
- Key 10 was generally weak, including a 41.38% 2025 result.
- Key 14 was mixed and did not show the same persistence as key 6.

This is a useful falsification result: broadening V1 from exact CROSS6 to a generic “cross a football key number” rule is not justified by this first shadow pass.

## 3+ book confirmation

For exact key 6, 3+ book confirmation stayed directionally similar to the unfiltered key-6 lane in the historical seasons, but sample reduction is meaningful. The current evidence does not justify assuming that 3+ books is independently causal or superior; it remains confirmation metadata until forward evidence proves otherwise.

## Movement magnitude and role splits

Some season-level bins show extreme hit rates, but many are small-N and unstable across years. No movement-magnitude or home-role interaction is promoted from this run. In particular:

- do not revive the rejected V1 dog/movement screen;
- do not promote a 3+ movement bucket from isolated strong seasons;
- do not use 2026 processed-season descriptive rows as a substitute for the frozen prospective ledger.

## Interpretation

The strongest takeaway from this first V2 pass is **specificity, not expansion**: exact key-6 behavior has been more persistent than the neighboring key classes, while most sub-strata remain too unstable or too small to promote.

Frozen V1 stays unchanged. Next V2 work should focus on stability/falsification across the preregistered strata, with 2025 kept separate and any future candidate requiring a new written freeze plus untouched validation.
